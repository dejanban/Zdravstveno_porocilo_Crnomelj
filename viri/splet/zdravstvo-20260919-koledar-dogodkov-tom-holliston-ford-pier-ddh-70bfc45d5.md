# Tom Holliston, Ford Pier, Ddh

## Zajeto besedilo

November se začneja z oldskul poslastico.
V MKK prihajata legendi in legendica, bivša člana NOMEANSNO, D.O.A., FORD PIER in TOM HOLLISTON!
Kot support se nam bo tudi premierno predstavil DOMEN DON HOLC.
//TOM HOLLISTON
Tom Holliston is a self-taught independent rock musician and songwriter from Lund, British Columbia, Canada. He’s probably best-known internationally for his 16 years touring and recording with legendary Canadian bands Nomeansno and the Hanson Brothers. Tom’s solo work (under his own name and his band Show Business Giants) generally consists of alternative rock songs, characterized by considerable musical variance with playful and obscure pop culture themes. On-stage he displays a singular blend of charm, humour, and social commentary.

//FORD PIER
Vancouver musician Ford Pier has released six solo albums, written and arranged for symphony orchestra and chamber ensembles, been published in Geist magazine, composed electronic soundscapes for fashion shows, performed as a member of D.O.A.,The Rheostatics, and Jr. Gone Wild, written songs with Matin Tielli, Neko Case, and the members of NoMeansNo, as well as recording and touring with Veda Hille, Carolyn Mark, and many others. His last record, Inclusive Fitness, was made with his string quartet, Strength Of Materials, and his aggro-/indie-/alt-rock band The Vengeance Trio just released their new album, Expensive Tissue, recorded by Dave Carswell (Destroyer, The Smugglers.)

//DDH - Domen Don Holc
Moje ime je Domen Don Holc, star sem 25 let in prihajam iz Zagorja ob Savi. Ampak se zadnjih 8 let večinoma nahajam v Ljubljani.
Igram v Bendu Koala Voice, pišem neke pesmi, in iz duše poskušam sodelovati pri glasbenih in gledališko - plesnih performansih.
Prijavljam se na vaš natečaj, medtem, ko smo z bendom v procesu touranja po Sloveniji in Evropi, ter snemanja komadov za ploščo. Tako da v priponkah pošiljam linke do svojega prvega solo komada Getting High With you, ter nastopa benda Koala Voice na izštekanih, kjer z njimi izvajam komad Frida Nipl.
V spodnjem we transfer linku pa še par komadov, ki bi jih zaenkrat ohranil neobjavljene, saj so del projekta snemanja plošče oziroma zaenkrat, live demo verzije, nekaj pa se jih pojavi tudi v filmu, Unstil/ Nemir, A. Kadunca, ki ima kljub predvajanju na letošnjem FSF- ju v Portorožu, Ljubljansko premiero šele čez par mesecev.
V prihodnosti me z bendom čaka par nastopov v Sloveniji, na festivalu Rad bi bil normalen v Ravnah na Cerknem, ter na Hrvaškem, Zagreb, Šibenik, z zaključnim koncertom na Split film festivalu, kjer smo Koala Voice leta 2017 dobili nagrado za najboljši videospot, s komadom Vede Premikanja.
3. Novembra pa me čaka samostojni koncert v MKK Črnomelj, kjer bom nastopil pred Tom Hollistonom, nekoč članom Kanadske zasedbe Nomeansno.
V oktobru snemam tudi komad Tvoj spomin, za projekt Andreja Šifrerja, ob 40. obletnici njegovega albuma Moj žulj, (1978).
Prilagam pa še par fotk iz nedeljskih fotoshootingov z Claudi Sovre Mikeljem, ter par drugimi prijatelji.
