Overview
Supporting tobacco users to quit is core demand-reduction

measure of the WHO Framework Convention on Tobacco Control (WHO FCTC) and a key

component of WHO MPOWER policy package. This guideline provides technical

guidance on a thorough set of tobacco cessation interventions for adults, and

to support WHO Member States to use evidence-based behavioural interventions

and pharmacological treatments for tobacco cessation as part of a comprehensive

tobacco control approach.
This guideline is primarily intended for use by health care

providers working in clinical and community settings where tobacco users may

present and be managed. The recommendations on system-level interventions and

policies contained in this guideline are to inform policy makers and health

service managers on how to enhance the adoption and implementation of effective

tobacco cessation interventions.
